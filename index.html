Fablemare — Horror legends from around the world

Watch on YouTube

Every culture has a monster it warns its children about.

Fablemare retells those warnings. Each episode takes an old folk legend and turns it into a short animated horror story, told with respect for the culture it comes from. We start with the legends of the Arabian Gulf and travel outward from there.

Umm Al Duwais

The woman who smells of oud, and the men who follow her.

Bu Darya

The Father of the Sea, who climbs aboard for the sailors who fall asleep.

More coming

New legends every week on Shorts, with full episodes on the channel.

About the studio

Fablemare is an independent channel. Stories are original retellings; visuals, narration and music are made with the help of AI tools and edited by hand. Videos are labelled as altered or synthetic content on YouTube.
